Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: To be set at release

Nordic Labs Tech Oy is responsible for ESIVA and is the controller for personal data it processes in connection with ESIVA. This Privacy Policy covers the esiva.app website and the current ESIVA application. Privacy contact: support@esiva.app.

ESIVA is currently in development. This policy describes the application planned for public release. It distinguishes the existing essential local Apple Health and external AI Nutrition processing, and the existing optional Story AI Processing, Voice Training AI Processing and Apple Music AI Processing, from planned optional analytics, which are not yet operating.

1. Plain-English Summary

  • ESIVA is designed to use health and fitness information to provide fitness and general wellness features — not advertising.
  • We do not intend to sell Health data or use it for advertising.
  • Apple Health permissions remain under your control.
  • App Store billing is handled by Apple when an Apple subscription is used.
  • Core planning uses information on your device. AI Nutrition sends the selected categories described below to OpenAI; it is not entirely on-device.
  • Essential Health & AI Data Processing consent (C2, version 2) is required for normal App access. It covers permitted Apple Health data processed locally on iPhone and selected nutrition information sent to OpenAI. Refusal or withdrawal blocks normal access and new covered processing, but does not automatically delete data or cancel subscriptions.
  • Story AI Processing (C6) is a separate optional purpose: selected photos are sent to OpenAI for Story creation. You can decline or withdraw it without losing access to other ESIVA features or existing Story archives.
  • Voice Training AI Processing (C7) is optional. Apple Speech converts voice input to text and may process it outside your device. OpenAI interprets your reviewed workout description; ESIVA builds the workout locally. Declining C7 does not disable other training features.
  • Apple Music AI Processing (C8) is optional. Selected musical information is sent to OpenAI for music calibration, public playlist classification and personalized music-library planning. Withdrawal stops new Music AI processing; saved selections remain available for playback when no new AI request is needed.
  • App-usage and health/outcome analytics are separate optional choices (C3 and C4), both off during initial onboarding. Their controls exist; analytical collection, transmission and server processing do not yet operate.
  • The current App has no user accounts, public profiles, Journey sharing, following, social graph, E Coins marketplace, creator earnings or active Outcome Network.

2. On-Device Data and Direct Contacts

2.1 Health and Fitness Information

With valid C2 consent and the separate system permissions, ESIVA accesses selected information available through Apple Health and connected Apple devices for the features you use. Permitted Apple Health information is processed locally on your iPhone.

This may include categories such as:

  • workouts and activity;
  • movement and active-energy information;
  • heart-rate-related information;
  • sleep and recovery-related information;
  • other health or fitness categories that are clearly requested and relevant to an ESIVA feature.

ESIVA uses this context for personalized training, recovery assessment, activity and energy balance, fitness and physiological calculations, daily planning and personalized recommendations on your device. Granting Apple Health permission does not mean that all health data is uploaded to ESIVA servers or OpenAI. The specific external AI categories are described in section 5.

2.2 Nutrition and User-Entered Information

Features may allow you to enter or record profile information, preferences, goals, meals, nutrition, weight, hunger, energy, training feedback and manual recovery or glucose information. These user-entered records are distinct from measurements received from Apple Health or Apple Watch and from indicators calculated locally by ESIVA.

ESIVA uses these inputs to provide the feature requested and, where applicable, improve personalization of future guidance on your device. Food photos, descriptions and selected nutrition context can also be used by AI Nutrition as described in section 5. This is distinct from optional sharing for broader recommendation improvement.

2.3 Subscription Status

If ESIVA offers an App Store subscription, the App may use Apple's StoreKit mechanisms to determine whether relevant paid functionality is available to you.

2.4 Support Communications

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address and the contents of your message.

2.5 Acceptance, Consent and Optional Choices

ESIVA records acceptance of the current Terms of Use (C1) separately from C2 consent. Terms acceptance evidence includes the accepted Terms version, acceptance time, App version and build, locale and the Privacy Policy version presented. C2 evidence also records the consent purpose and version, consent-text version, granted or withdrawn state, relevant dates and how the consent was provided or withdrawn. C1 and C2 evidence is held in separate Keychain records on your iPhone, not in an ESIVA account. C3 and C4 are two independent choices saved in local App settings; they do not currently create analytical records or a server-side analytics dataset.

Optional C6 Story AI consent has its own separate Keychain evidence. It does not replace C2 or change C3/C4 choices.

Optional C7 Voice Training AI consent has a separate, versioned Keychain record on your iPhone. It records the purpose, consent-copy version, timestamp, granted, declined or withdrawn decision, App version and build, and locale. No record means consent has not been requested. Optional C8 Apple Music AI consent has its own versioned Keychain record on your iPhone, separate from C1–C7. It records the purpose, consent-copy version, timestamp, granted, declined or withdrawn decision, App version and build, and locale. No record means consent has not been requested.

5. Essential Health & AI Data Processing

C2 covers two essential activities: local processing of permitted Apple Health data on iPhone for the functions described in section 2.1, and selected AI Nutrition processing through OpenAI. Both are necessary for ESIVA's designed personalized health, training and nutrition service. Only the selected nutrition information described below is sent to OpenAI; local Apple Health processing does not mean that all Health measurements are transmitted externally.

Within C2, AI Nutrition sends requests from your iPhone to OpenAI to estimate nutrition, suggest remaining-day meals and create meal images. OpenAI receives the relevant request; the App displays the returned nutrition estimate, meal proposal or generated image locally.

Food photos

A food photo and any optional text hint are sent for analysis. A hint may be typed or transcribed from speech. Analysis can start after selecting or taking a photo; a retry can send a new request. Story-specific food analysis is described separately under C6.

Food descriptions

When you request analysis, your typed food or drink description, or the text obtained from speech recognition, is sent. An audio recording is not included in these OpenAI nutrition requests. This statement does not describe the separate speech-recognition service's processing.

Remaining-day meal proposals

The request uses selected nutrition context: calorie, protein, fat, carbohydrate and fibre targets, logged values and remaining requirements for individual meals and the remaining day. It also includes summaries of foods recorded during the previous 14 completed days, such as foods, quantities, nutrients and how often they were recorded; pseudonymous device-local and request identifiers; and day, time-zone and timing context. Pseudonymous does not mean anonymous.

Proposals can be requested with Generate or automatically when the Today/Lab nutrition context is ready and eligible during an active App session. External processing therefore does not always require a separate button press for each request.

Generated meal images

Image requests use the AI-proposed dish name, ingredients, quantities, units and brief preparation information. They do not send your original food photo or the broader nutrition context. Requests follow successful meal proposals automatically; a missing local cached image can cause a further request.

What the App does not automatically add

These AI Nutrition requests do not automatically include raw Apple Health samples, blood glucose, menstrual-cycle data, training plans, your name, email address or an account ID. Nutrition targets can reflect a broader calculation performed locally. Photos, hints and descriptions can themselves contain personal information you choose to include.

Consent and control

During initial onboarding, all four C1–C4 checkboxes are unchecked. Accepting the current Terms (C1) and explicitly selecting the separate C2 checkbox are required to continue. Reading this policy is informational and has no acceptance checkbox. You may refuse C2; without valid consent, normal ESIVA access is unavailable.

To withdraw C2, open Settings → Consent Center → Withdraw Essential Data Consent. Withdrawal blocks normal App access, new AI requests covered by C2 and new covered iPhone-initiated HealthKit authorization, reading and refresh operations. It does not revoke Apple's system permissions, reset C3/C4 choices, automatically delete existing local data or erase information already sent to OpenAI. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; subscription management remains separate. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.

You may explicitly provide consent again through the required consent screen. C1 and C2 are again unchecked and require your selection; C3 and C4 restore your persisted optional choices. Valid current Terms acceptance and C2 consent restore normal access and permit new processing through the existing checks, subject to separate system permissions where required.

Optional app usage sharing (C3)

Share App Usage Data is an implemented optional choice, off by default during initial onboarding and independently changeable in Consent Center. Its current effect is to save your choice locally. Planned C3 analytics would use limited product-usage and technical events to understand feature use, performance and reliability, not health histories or detailed nutrition content. Analytical observation collection, transmission to an ESIVA analytics backend and server-side storage or processing are not yet implemented.

Optional health and outcome sharing (C4)

Share Health & Outcome Data is a separate implemented optional choice, also off by default during initial onboarding and independently changeable in Consent Center. It currently saves only your local choice. Planned C4 analytics would use selected health, training, nutrition and outcome information to improve recommendations, study outcomes and learn what works for people with similar profiles or goals. No C4 analytical collection, upload, server-side dataset or processing is implemented. This is distinct from local personalization, C2 and public Journey sharing.

C3 and C4 are independent of each other and are not conditions for normal ESIVA access. Refusing either does not limit normal App functionality; withdrawing C2 does not reset them. Before either analytical flow starts, we will provide its approved data categories, purposes, recipients, storage region, retention, security and access controls, and user identification and rights procedures. Any required notice or consent will precede the processing. Once such a flow is active, switching its choice off will prevent new collection and transfer for that channel; deletion of earlier records will follow the applicable separate procedure.

The planned analytical backend is not an App backup, synchronization or recovery service, or a source for App calculations. Any future server retention will be independent of local retention; regular analytical transmission will not wait for local records to expire.

Outcome Network (C5) is not implemented and receives no data in the current product.

5.1. Story AI Processing (C6)

Story AI Processing is an implemented, optional purpose for creating Stories from photos you select. OpenAI receives the selected photo and processing instructions for the relevant operation. Photos may contain people. Depending on the Story workflow, processing can include the following operations; not every Story uses all three:

  • Photo classification. The selected photo is sent to OpenAI to classify its content as person, food or uncertain. The result guides the Story workflow; classification does not establish a person's identity.
  • AI photo processing. The selected photo is sent to OpenAI with image-processing instructions reflecting the content category and selected ESIVA theme, to return a processed or generated Story image. The photo may show people.
  • Story food analysis. For applicable food Stories, including when proceeding to Metrics, OpenAI analyzes the selected photo and returns an estimated food description, calories and nutrients. This Story-specific operation is separate from ordinary AI Nutrition food logging under C2.

These requests also carry technical request identifiers. Results are used locally within the Story workflow; saved Stories belong to the separate local archive described in section 12. C6 does not create a C3/C4 analytics dataset or a new ESIVA server store of your photos.

Explicit consent before Story creation

C6 is not requested during onboarding. When you select Proceed to Photo without a valid C6 grant, ESIVA opens the Story AI Processing consent screen before photo selection or commercial preparation and reservation for a new Story. It explains OpenAI processing and links to this policy. Its checkbox starts unchecked; Continue is enabled only after you select it, while Decline is always available. Opening Story or this screen does not itself grant consent.

An explicit grant is saved and read back successfully from a separate local Keychain record before processing is authorized. This versioned evidence records the purpose, consent-copy version, timestamp, decision, App version and build, and locale. Missing or invalid evidence, or a storage failure, does not authorize processing. A successful grant resumes the requested Story action; later attempts proceed without another prompt while the grant remains valid.

Decline closes the consent screen without starting Story processing or a commercial reservation. Proceed to Photo remains available regardless of C6 state, subject to the existing photo and commercial requirements. If consent has not been requested, was declined or was withdrawn, the next attempt opens the same unchecked consent screen. There is no mandatory redirection to Settings. A valid grant continues the normal Story flow.

Independent authorization and withdrawal

Every new Story AI request, including a retry, requires valid C2 consent, valid C6 consent and the applicable commercial authorization. C6 does not replace C2 or expand its mandatory purpose, and is not required for normal ESIVA access. Ordinary AI Nutrition does not require C6.

You can review, withdraw or grant C6 again independently in Settings → Consent Center. Withdrawal prevents new Story AI requests until you explicitly grant consent again, either there or directly from Story. It does not withdraw C2, change C3/C4 choices, cancel an Apple subscription, automatically delete existing Stories or erase information already sent to OpenAI. Existing Story archives remain accessible. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.

5.2. Voice Training AI Processing (C7)

Voice Training AI Processing is an independent optional purpose for understanding the workout you request. You may describe a workout by voice or type a description. Your description and answers may contain personal or health-related information you choose to share.

Speech recognition, interpretation and workout generation

For voice input, Apple Speech converts your speech into text, subject to the separate iOS microphone and speech-recognition permissions. Speech recognition may involve processing outside your device; ESIVA does not require exclusively on-device recognition. You can review and edit the recognized text. Local typing and editing do not themselves send a request to OpenAI.

When you submit a request for AI interpretation, ESIVA sends your reviewed workout description directly from your iPhone to OpenAI's Responses API. The request may also include clarification questions, your answers and static ESIVA training vocabulary. OpenAI returns an interpretation of your workout intent, a request for clarification or an indication that the request cannot be supported.

This Voice Training request does not automatically include raw microphone audio, stored Apple Health or HealthKit records, Recovery values, Training Load values, current weight, age, your stored profile or workout history. The text you choose to submit can nevertheless contain such information. ESIVA builds the actual workout using its existing local training logic, which may use Recovery, Training Load and other applicable local inputs. Using that context locally does not add it to the OpenAI request.

Voice Training requests are configured with OpenAI response storage turned off. This request setting does not guarantee zero provider retention or the absence of operational logs. Provider processing is described in section 10; local data lifecycles are described in section 12.

Separate consent and control

C7 is not requested during onboarding. If you select Describe by Voice without a valid grant, ESIVA opens the Voice Training AI Processing disclosure before microphone recording or speech recognition starts. Its checkbox starts unchecked; Continue requires your explicit selection, while Decline remains available. The screen also links to this policy. For a typed request, consent is checked before external interpretation and commercial admission, without blocking local typing or editing.

A grant must be saved and successfully read back before it authorizes processing. Missing or invalid evidence, or a storage failure, does not authorize a request. A successful grant resumes the original action once; subsequent attempts proceed without another prompt while it remains valid. Decline records your decision and closes the screen without starting the pending recording or AI processing. You can try again after declining or withdrawing: the same unchecked consent screen opens, without requiring a visit to Settings.

Every new Voice Training AI request, including a retry, requires valid C7 consent, valid C2 authorization and the applicable commercial authorization. C7 does not replace or expand C2, grant paid access, or authorize AI Nutrition or Story AI Processing. Declining C7 leaves other training features available under their existing requirements.

In Settings → Consent Center, you can review your C7 status and disclosure, withdraw consent or explicitly grant it again. Withdrawal prevents new Voice Training AI requests until you grant consent again, either there or directly from Voice Training. It does not change C1–C6, cancel a subscription, automatically delete saved workouts or erase information already processed by Apple Speech or sent to OpenAI. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.

5.3. Apple Music AI Processing (C8)

Music 2.0 uses separate optional Apple Music AI Processing consent (C8) to personalize music calibration and music-library planning through OpenAI. C8 is not part of onboarding and is not required for normal ESIVA access or unrelated features. It does not authorize AI Nutrition, Story, Voice Training or other AI purposes. Normal App access remains subject to C1 and C2, and existing commercial requirements remain separate.

Music sources and AI processing

With your separate Apple Music authorization, ESIVA can retrieve limited samples of songs in your library, recently played songs and Apple Music recommendations. It constructs a bounded listening context rather than sending your entire library or complete listening history to OpenAI. The initial context contains up to 12 deduplicated selections represented by track title, artist name and source category. These observations are not necessarily songs you have explicitly marked as favourites.

Soundcharts is a separate external music-data provider supplying music catalog, public playlist and song-level acoustic information. OpenAI assists with interpretation, classification and discovery planning. ESIVA maintains calibration evidence, music preferences, saved profiles and candidate music-library membership locally on your iPhone; OpenAI does not maintain your persistent ESIVA music profile for the App.

The three active Music AI operations are:

  • Personal music calibration. When you start calibration or recalibration, selected listening observations and track information support AI nominations. Adaptive requests also use your comparison answers, positive and negative music choices, genres, playlist and recording identifiers, and song-level acoustic characteristics to refine the musical preference evidence used by ESIVA.
  • Public playlist classification. During music discovery, OpenAI classifies public Apple Music playlist metadata: the playlist identifier, name and optional description. This operation does not send your private listening history.
  • Music-library formation and updates. When ESIVA forms or updates your personalized music library, AI-assisted search planning uses existing calibration and comparison evidence, genre, source and regional preferences, exclusions and other discovery constraints. Requests may also include selected song and artist information, recording and playlist identifiers, previous queries and search outcomes, Apple Music search/verification feedback and relevant library-formation progress. Previously collected calibration evidence can therefore be used in subsequent requests to form or update your music library.

These Music AI requests do not automatically include Apple Music authentication tokens or account identifiers, device identifiers, HealthKit records, Recovery or Training Load values, workout history, physiological workout measurements, calorie data, stored non-music personal profile information or audio recordings. Musical acoustic characteristics describe songs, not your physiology. Musical preference information is not necessarily anonymous merely because direct account identifiers are absent. These boundaries apply to Music AI requests, not to the other AI services described in this policy.

Your consent and Apple Music authorization

Before starting AI calibration or a library operation that needs C8, ESIVA presents the Apple Music AI Processing disclosure if a valid current grant is absent. The checkbox starts unchecked; Continue requires your explicit selection and successful saving and verification of your consent. Decline cancels the pending attempt without starting Apple Music acquisition or AI processing. You can try again and reopen the same unchecked screen. Previous Music AI grants do not automatically satisfy the current C8 consent. While your current grant remains valid, it need not be repeated for each operation, but every new C8-scoped OpenAI request, including a retry, requires current authorization.

C8 is separate from Apple's MusicKit permission to access Apple Music. Neither permission replaces the other. Apple Music access and playback remain subject to the applicable Apple system permissions and service availability.

Withdrawal, re-consent and saved playback

You can review your C8 status, withdraw consent or explicitly grant it again in Settings → Consent Center. You can also grant consent again directly when starting calibration or a Music AI operation. Declined or withdrawn consent does not require a visit to Settings before retrying. Withdrawal prevents new AI calibration, recalibration, playlist classification, music-library formation, updates and other C8-scoped OpenAI requests until you grant consent again.

Withdrawal does not automatically delete your calibrated profile, saved music preferences, generated selections or existing music-library membership. Saved results remain available for ordinary playback and local ranking when no new Music AI request is needed, subject to Apple Music authorization. Withdrawal does not revoke that authorization, change C1–C7 or cancel subscriptions. It does not reverse processing already completed by OpenAI or automatically erase previously transmitted information. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal; applicable erasure and other rights remain available as described in section 13. Local records and provider retention have separate lifecycles, as explained in sections 10 and 12.

11. Consent and Legal Requirements

We distinguish the lawful basis under Article 6 GDPR from the additional condition needed for special-category health data under Article 9:

  • Necessary contractual processing outside C2, C6, C7 and C8, where applicable, relies on Article 6(1)(b). This includes recording acceptance of the Terms (C1) and necessary service operations outside those consent-based purposes. C1 is not health-data consent.
  • C2 relies on consent under Article 6(1)(a). For health data within C2, explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) is the additional condition. Article 9 does not replace Article 6, and local processing is not exempt from GDPR simply because information stays on your device.
  • Planned C3 and C4 analytics are separate optional consent-based purposes under Article 6(1)(a); C4 health-data processing will additionally require explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a). Neither C1, C2 nor Apple Health permissions authorizes these separate purposes. No C5 processing is active.
  • C6 Story AI Processing is a separate optional purpose based on consent under Article 6(1)(a). Where the actual processing involves special-category personal data, explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) is the additional condition. A photograph is not automatically health or biometric data simply because it shows a person.
  • C7 Voice Training AI Processing is a separate optional purpose based on consent under Article 6(1)(a). Where the submitted description or answers involve special-category personal data, including health information, explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) is the additional condition.
  • C8 Apple Music AI Processing is a separate optional purpose based on consent under Article 6(1)(a). It concerns the musical information described in section 5.3; song-level acoustic characteristics are not physiological measurements, and the Music AI requests do not automatically include health data.

C2 is necessary for ESIVA's designed personalized service, with the refusal and withdrawal consequences explained in section 5. Consent must meet GDPR's requirements, including the assessment of service necessity under Article 7(4).

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, valid legal process or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, Nordic Labs Tech Oy or others.

Uninstalling ESIVA removes its active local App container; it is not a request or guarantee to delete Apple Health data, Keychain evidence, CloudKit commercial records or information already processed by Apple Speech or sent to OpenAI. Device backups have a separate lifecycle. Provider retention and deletion depend on the applicable service, configuration and data-processing terms, not on the local limits above. CloudKit is described separately in section 10.
