14 — GLUCOSE FORECAST

How might your glucose
respond to this meal?

ARC Engine starts with a reference profile informed by our in-house research. It estimates when your glucose may peak after a meal and when it may dip later in the day. As you log measured readings, ESIVA uses your own responses to refine future forecasts and suggestions.

Forecasts are estimates for everyday planning. Use measured readings and your clinician’s advice for diabetes care or treatment decisions.
