How it works

From raw signals to a practical day.

ESIVA is built around a simple loop: observe, interpret, recommend and learn.

  1. 1

    Read the day

    Bring together the available signals from your activity, recovery and habits.

  2. 2

    Understand the context

    ARC Engine evaluates what those signals mean together, not in isolation.

  3. 3

    Choose the next action

    Receive clear guidance for training, food timing and recovery.

  4. 4

    Adapt as the day changes

    New activity, meals and feedback can update what comes next.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

What is ESIVA?

ESIVA is a fitness and wellness application designed to connect training, recovery and nutrition into clear daily guidance.

What is ARC Engine?

ARC Engine is the intelligence layer behind ESIVA. It is designed to interpret changing signals and translate them into practical recommendations.

Which platforms are planned?

The initial product is being developed for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Is ESIVA a medical product?

No. ESIVA is intended for fitness and general wellness guidance. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

When will ESIVA be available?

The application is currently in development. The App Store link and release information will be published here when available.
