05 — JOURNEY
IN DEVELOPMENT · BUILDING THE FOUNDATION

Real journeys.
A path closer
to yours.

A future community built on positive motivation and real progress. ESIVA is being designed to connect you with anonymous journeys from people with a similar profile who have reached a goal like yours.

Statistical matching would help you explore the training patterns, food habits and consistency behind their progress — and find a path relevant to your own starting point.

Learn from a life

FOR THE PEOPLE WHO GO FIRST

Your progress could
help someone else begin.

The launch phase focuses on accumulating journey history. Early participants can build a record of their progress, with the future opportunity to contribute an anonymous journey.

A PLANNED REWARD PROGRAM

A path to contribution.
A possibility of rewards.

The planned algorithm would select credible, successful journeys. Contributors could earn financial rewards when their selected journeys are used by others, subject to the future program’s eligibility rules and terms.

Recommendations and rewards are not available yet. Launch timing, journey selection, participation terms and payment details will be announced separately. Participation does not guarantee selection or earnings.
